Heart of Harmony by CKPC Properties is a premium 24-storey residential high-rise at Kudlu Gate, Hosur Main Road (NH48), Bengaluru 560068. The project offers 137 exclusive 3 BHK, 3.5 BHK and 4 BHK sky residences with only 6 homes per floor, zero common walls, and 270° panoramic views. Located 400 metres from Kudlu Gate Metro Station (Yellow Line) with possession scheduled for March 2030. RERA: PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/310326/008560.
