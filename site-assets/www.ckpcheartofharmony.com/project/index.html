FAQ's

Frequently Asked Questions — Heart of Harmony Project

What is the master plan of Heart of Harmony?

The Heart of Harmony master plan features a 2 basement + ground + 24-floor tower on a vehicle-free podium with 84% open green space. Only 6 residences per floor with zero common walls, private sky garden terraces every three floors, and 2 levels of amenities on ground podium and rooftop terrace.

What amenities are available at Heart of Harmony?

Heart of Harmony offers 40+ amenities across two levels: Wellness & Play (basketball court, outdoor gym, pickleball, jogging track, bicycle track, rock climbing wall, table tennis, indoor games), Relaxation (sky deck pool, spa, sauna, steam, jacuzzi, ice bath), Social Experience (amphitheatre, banquet hall, co-working space), and Indulgence amenities.

Where is Heart of Harmony located?

Heart of Harmony is located at Kudlu Gate on Hosur Main Road (NH48), Bengaluru 560068. It is 400 metres from Kudlu Gate Metro Station (Yellow Line) and 10 minutes from Silk Board junction, with easy access to Koramangala, HSR Layout, BTM Layout and Electronic City.

What is the possession date of Heart of Harmony?

Heart of Harmony possession is scheduled for March 2030 as per RERA registration number PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/310326/008560. All construction milestones are governed by the RERA filing, verifiable at rera.karnataka.gov.in.

Is Heart of Harmony RERA registered?

Yes. Heart of Harmony is RERA registered under PRM/KA/RERA/1251/310/PR/310326/008560. Verify at rera.karnataka.gov.in.
